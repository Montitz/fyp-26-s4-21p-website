What the app will do

A mobile chat for children and parents, plus a web portal for administrators. The app is in development, and screens and a demo will appear here as each sprint finishes.

How a request works

  1. Ask. The child types a topic or taps a suggested reply.
  2. Filter. Rules remove anything outside the child’s age or the parent’s limits. This happens before any scoring.
  3. Rank. A pretrained language model and a small neural ranker order what is left by how well it matches.
  4. Explain. Each result shows why it was chosen, such as the matching topics and age fit.

Our design choices

These are the choices we are building around. We are still checking how they compare with existing services, so we state them as design goals, not proven advantages.

  • Books and videos in one chat, instead of separate apps.
  • Parent controls for each child, including blocked topics and how deep a topic can go at each age.
  • A reason for every result, so parents and children can see why something was suggested.
  • Reviewed content only. The app recommends from a curated catalogue and does not invent titles.
